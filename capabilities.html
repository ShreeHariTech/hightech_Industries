MANUFACTURING DISCIPLINE

ENGINEERING CAPABILITIES

Technical overview of our machinery fabrication, hydraulic assembly, and load testing capabilities in Ahmedabad.

DISCIPLINE 01

HYDRAULIC EQUIPMENT

Our hydraulic machinery manufacturing includes 100 Ton to 150 Ton force motorized compression press machines, standalone hydraulic press heads, and 3HP electric hydraulic power packs. Built with structural mild steel frames to withstand continuous field pressing operations.

TECHNICAL PARAMETERS:

Press Tonnage: 100T - 150T • Motor Drive: 3HP 3-Phase Electric • High Pressure Hydraulic Seals

DISCIPLINE 02

CABLE HANDLING EQUIPMENT

Engineering of mechanical screw drum jacks rated from 7 Ton to 8 Ton capacity, constructed with heavy structural mild steel legs and trapezoidal screw rods for smooth cable reel elevation and payout.

TECHNICAL PARAMETERS:

Load Rating: 7 Ton - 8 Ton per pair • Mechanism: Trapezoidal Mechanical Screw • Wide Base

DISCIPLINE 03

TRANSMISSION LINE TOOLS

Fabrication of motorized pulling winches and overhead conductor stringing tools engineered for high-tension traction in utility terrain.

TECHNICAL PARAMETERS:

Structure: Steel Skid Base • Traction: Motorized Winch • Control: Fwd/Rev Mechanical Clutch

DISCIPLINE 04

CUTTING & CRIMPING

Precision CNC machining of hardened tool steel hexagonal compressor die sets, forged steel compress heads, and high-carbon alloy steel wire cutter hubs.

TECHNICAL PARAMETERS:

Material: Hardened Tool Steel / Forged Alloy • Profile: Hexagonal & Round • High Carbon

DISCIPLINE 05

INDUSTRIAL MACHINES

Fabrication of alloy steel box-frame hydraulic press machines for workshop maintenance and mobile light tower generators for project site illumination.
