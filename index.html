FIELD SECTORS

APPLICATIONS WE SERVE

Our machinery is engineered to support demanding field infrastructure and power projects.

POWER TRANSMISSION

Conductor jointing and sleeve compression for EHV overhead transmission line towers using 150T motorized compression units.

CABLE INSTALLATION

Heavy drum elevation, unwinding, and continuous tension pulling for heavy electrical cables during utility trenching.

ELECTRICAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Substation terminal crimping, busbar jointing, and high pressure lug pressing using standalone hydraulic compress heads.

INDUSTRIAL CONSTRUCTION

Heavy alloy press operations, component forming, and stay wire cutting using hardened wire cutter hubs.

UTILITY PROJECTS

Continuous field illumination for night infrastructure work using mobile light tower generator sets.

HEAVY ENGINEERING

High precision tool steel die pressing and custom hydraulic RAM applications for industrial plant maintenance.
