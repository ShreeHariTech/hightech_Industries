OVERVIEW

MANUFACTURING OBJECTIVE

High Tech Industries, established in 2017 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, is engaged in manufacturing hydraulic equipment, cable handling equipment and specialized industrial machinery. The company offers equipment used across cable installation, power transmission and industrial applications.

Located in the major industrial corridor of Ahmedabad, our manufacturing facility combines structural mild steel fabrication, high-pressure hydraulic assembly, and load testing to meet field reliability standards.
